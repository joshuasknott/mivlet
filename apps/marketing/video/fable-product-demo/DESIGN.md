# Fable product demo

## Style Prompt

A precise, monochrome desktop workspace in Fable's current black-and-white visual system. The interface should feel calm, capable, and local-first: dark graphite surfaces, white type, thin neutral rules, compact product chrome, and restrained motion that makes delegated work and explicit approvals legible without marketing theatrics.

## Colors

- `#050505` — canvas and deepest background
- `#0D0D0E` — application shell
- `#151516` — raised controls and task surfaces
- `#303034` — borders and dividers
- `#FFFFFF` — primary type and active controls
- `#A6A6AB` — secondary type

## Typography

- Geist — interface and display text
- JetBrains Mono — metadata, labels, and timestamps

## Motion

- Confident, short entrances with varied direction and easing
- One slow horizontal drift through the task board
- A cursor demonstrates one approval and one completed handoff
- No bounces, elastic easing, or decorative zoom loops

## What NOT to Do

- No copper, orange, gradients, glow, or saturated accents
- No fake analytics, testimonials, or vanity metrics
- No generic glassmorphism or oversized rounded cards
- No infinite repeats or random motion
- No tiny web-scale typography
