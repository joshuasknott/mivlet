# Arden Brand

## Name

Arden is the product and project name. Use `Arden` in product UI, documentation, release notes, and public-facing copy.

## Positioning

Arden is an open-source AI workspace for real work. It lets people chat with their computer, delegate tasks across tools and files, and stay in control at every step.

Local-first and private by default, Arden is built for people who want powerful AI assistance without giving up ownership of their work.

Short tagline: Open AI workspace for real work.

## Logo

The logo system uses a refined A inside an open workspace frame. It should feel calm, precise, and controlled, with no robot, brain, circuit, or sci-fi motifs.

Use these assets:

- `apps/desktop/public/brand/arden-mark.svg`
- `apps/desktop/public/brand/arden-wordmark.svg`
- `apps/desktop/public/brand/arden-logo.svg`
- `apps/desktop/public/brand/arden-logo-dark.svg`

Prefer the full lockup when space allows. Use the mark alone for favicons, app icons, compact navigation, and small surfaces. Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one quarter of the mark width.

## Colour

The logo must work in black and white first. The current palette uses graphite, warm off-white, and a restrained grey-green accent:

- Graphite: `#17191A`
- Warm canvas: `#F6F2EA`
- Muted accent: `#8B948D`

Avoid bright gradients, saturated blues, neon effects, and decorative glow.

## Voice

Arden should sound direct, calm, and serious. Prefer plain language about control, ownership, privacy, permissions, tools, files, and real work.

Avoid hype words such as revolutionary, magic, supercharged, copilot, and agent army.
