# Grok Bot screenshot ledger

Captured from the signed-in Windows desktop app on 18 August 2026. The observed app reported version 0.18.0. Each image is an exact active-window capture at 1042 × 761; no screenshot was reconstructed. The settings captures may contain the signed-in account identity and should remain private.

## Boundaries of this walkthrough

- No connector, plugin, external account, or OAuth grant was added.
- No email, calendar, Git, Slack, Teams, Linear, Sentry, PagerDuty, or other external action was executed.
- No microphone or voice recording was started.
- No file was uploaded.
- No app/computer update, reset, cancellation, purchase, upgrade, sign-out, or deletion was executed.
- A `Researcher` Bot was created during onboarding. Inspecting the later creator also created `New Bot`; inspecting organization created an empty `New section`. Both were left because the app treats deletion as an irreversible account mutation.
- Accessibility inspection covered the reachable controls and labels, but this is not a screen-reader or keyboard-only conformance audit.
